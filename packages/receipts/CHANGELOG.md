## 0.1.0

The first release. The shared shape of the delivery-app receipt servers (bolt-food and glovo): itemised receipts, the match from a bank card charge to the order it paid for, product search, spending per store and per month, CSV exports and the store of order details. A server built on it brings only its app's own API adapter and the words its tools use.
