## 0.1.0

The first release. Whether a name is already a registered or pending trademark: one search across offices (the USPTO, and EUIPO once it has credentials), narrowed by Nice class or by the goods and services a mark covers, closest first with a similarity score, and for a US mark every step of its file and its documents, saved as a PDF.

Every tool is `read`: it files nothing, and no tool is `money`. An office that could not be searched says why, so its silence is never read as a clear name.

It speaks MCP protocol revisions 2026-07-28 and 2025-11-25, over stdio and loopback HTTP, and lists the same tools to both.
