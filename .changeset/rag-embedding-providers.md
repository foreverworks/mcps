A collection can be indexed at Cloudflare Workers AI, which serves the same `BAAI/bge-m3` model, instead of the local embed endpoint: `create_collection` takes `embedding_provider` (`local`, the default, or `cloudflare`), proves with three probe texts that Workers AI embeds them as the local endpoint does (cosine at least 0.999) and refuses it otherwise, and moves an existing collection to the provider named without embedding again what it holds. Questions are always embedded locally. `index_path` embeds in batches of 64, retries Workers AI's capacity and rate-limit refusals with backoff and Retry-After, stops at once on a spent daily allocation with a `provider` error whose reason is `quota`, and reports the requests, texts, tokens and neurons each call cost; `list_collections` shows each collection's provider and its total spend, and `health` which providers are configured, without calling them. New settings: `RAG_CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID`, `RAG_CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN_FILE`, `RAG_CLOUDFLARE_MODEL`, `RAG_CLOUDFLARE_BATCH`. The write tools are the same four: `create_collection`, `index_path`, `remove_source` and `drop_collection`.
